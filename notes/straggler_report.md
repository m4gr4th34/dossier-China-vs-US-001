## 3. CN-1940-1 — wartime industrial relocation — **CONVERT-PROPOSAL (report only, NOT applied)**

- **Current claim (diffuse):** "China relocates key industries and universities to
  the southwestern interior (Chongqing, Sichuan) to sustain wartime production and
  scholarship." (range ~1937–1940, anchored ~1940)
- **Anchored-reformulation candidate:** the **1938 Yichang evacuation** — Lu Zuofu's
  **Minsheng Shipping Company** moved industrial equipment and personnel up the
  Yangtze through the Three Gorges gorges past Yichang before the city fell, in a
  roughly **40-day operation in late 1938** ("China's Dunkirk").
- **Feasibility:** **strong.** This converts a diffuse multi-year process into a
  **discrete, dateable event** with a named actor and a defined window — exactly the
  CN-1934-1 → Canton–Hankow and Ding County → Zouping precedents.
- **Sources consulted:** Lu Zuofu / Minsheng company histories (encyclopedic);
  *Recharging China in War and Revolution, 1882–1955* (Cornell UP — academic); a
  peer-reviewed PMC study on National Resources Commission defence-industry site
  selection (1937–1945); accounts of the Steel Plant Relocation Committee moving
  ~323,000 tons of equipment Wuhan→Chongqing via the Yichang bottleneck. **Note:**
  the widely-cited "1.5 million people / 1 million tons" figures trace partly to a
  non-evidentiary source (Minghui); the *industrial-equipment* evacuation is the
  verifiable core and should be what any promoted claim asserts.
- **Recommendation:** **CONVERT** CN-1940-1 → an anchored 1938 Yichang-evacuation
  row (category `industrial`, event_type `completion`/`milestone`, year 1938), then
  verify against the academic sources above under the standard ritual. Because
  conversion narrows/moves the claim, it needs **author sign-off** — proposed here,
  not applied.

- **Current claim (diffuse):** "Chinese-owned cotton-textile firms (e.g., the Rong
  family's Shenxin mills) become the leading modern manufacturing sector in the
  lower Yangtze." (circa 1930)
- **Crisp-anchor search:** no single discrete *event* (no clean "Chinese mills

- **Sources consulted:** Harvard Business School case *The Rong Family: Business
  Strategies and Survival in Modern China* (308-066 — independent-academic);
  scholarship on Rong Zongjing/Desheng and Shenxin (Wikipedia + academic PDFs on the
  Shenxin bank-consortium and Mill No. 7). These would **qualify** an anchored
  reformulation.
- **Recommendation:** **CONVERT-CANDIDATE** — reformulate to the anchored scale-fact
  ("by ~1932 the Rong family's Shenxin mills were China's largest industrial
  employer / largest private industrial group"), sourced to the HBS case + academic
  work; anchor ~1932, `industrial`/`milestone`. **Fallback: accept-permanently-open**
  if the author prefers to keep the broader "cotton-textile sector" framing rather
  than narrow the claim to one firm. **REMOVE is not recommended** — unlike
